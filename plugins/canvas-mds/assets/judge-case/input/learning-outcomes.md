# Approved learning outcomes

- RA1: Analyze digital transformation and value-creation opportunities in context.
- RA2: Diagnose and prioritize a problem using evidence.
- RA3: Design and prototype a data/AI-enabled solution with human oversight.
- RA4: Evaluate desirability, feasibility, viability, responsibility, and impact.
- RA5: Formulate governance, risk, adoption, and responsible scaling.
- RA6: Communicate and defend the proposal for different audiences.

These outcomes are approved. Their meaning and relative importance must not be rewritten by the redesign workflow.
