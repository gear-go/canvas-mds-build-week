# Institutional AI guidance

Generative AI may support exploration, critique, coding, prototyping, and communication when the instructor authorizes it.

Students remain accountable for:

- verifying claims and generated outputs;
- protecting confidential, personal, or sensitive data;
- documenting consequential uses of AI;
- identifying limitations and rejected AI suggestions;
- demonstrating their own understanding.

AI must not be used as an autonomous grader or as evidence of individual authorship.
