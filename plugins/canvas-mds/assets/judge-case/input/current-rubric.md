# Current final-project rubric

The final dossier, prototype, and presentation are judged on:

1. Quality and relevance of the diagnosed problem.
2. Coherence of the proposed value proposition.
3. Technical and organizational feasibility.
4. Responsible use of data and AI.
5. Governance, risks, adoption, and impact.
6. Clarity and persuasiveness of the final defense.

The rubric refers to critical reasoning, evidence, iteration, and responsible AI use, but it evaluates those qualities primarily through the final team artifact and presentation.
