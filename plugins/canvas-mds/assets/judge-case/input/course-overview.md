# Digital Innovation Studio · Judge case

This sanitized postgraduate course brings together approximately 45 students from data science, information systems, and management programs. Teams diagnose a real digital-transformation opportunity and design, prototype, validate, and defend a responsible data/AI-enabled solution.

The course intends to develop problem framing, evidence-based decisions, interdisciplinary collaboration, iterative prototyping, responsible AI use, governance, and the ability to defend consequential decisions.

The current Canvas design emphasizes the final dossier, prototype, and Demo Day. Faculty want to preserve the authentic project while making learning visible before the final submission.
