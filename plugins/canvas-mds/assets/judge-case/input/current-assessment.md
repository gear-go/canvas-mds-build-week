# Current product-centered assessment

| Assessment | Weight | Scope | Timing |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Final dossier and prototype | 70% | Team | End of course |
| Demo Day presentation | 25% | Team | End of course |
| Individual reflection | 5% | Individual | After Demo Day |

There are informal coaching conversations, but no graded milestones or required evidence showing how teams framed the problem, rejected alternatives, responded to feedback, verified AI output, or revised the prototype.

A strong final artifact can receive a high grade even when the instructor cannot determine how the learning process unfolded or who made the consequential decisions.
